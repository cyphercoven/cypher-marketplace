# Law reference: benefits

PURPOSE: source-based UK benefits legal research, entitlement decisions, mandatory reconsideration and welfare appeals.
CONVENTIONS: current legal facts come from live primary sources; no personal case material is bundled.
AGENT NOTES: verification history is in ../VERIFIED.md; it is provenance, not a current-law guarantee. The source and currency rules and the legal-information line are in ../SKILL.md and apply here.

## When to use
Load for UK benefits legal research, entitlement decisions, mandatory reconsideration and welfare appeals. Establish country, benefit, claimant category, decision date, stated reasons and challenge deadline.

## Work the issue
Distinguish entitlement law from operational guidance and discretionary support. Verify the applicable regulations and transitional cohort before calculating or stating a rate. Identify mandatory reconsideration and appeal routes from current official guidance. DWP guidance is not binding legislation.

## Starting lookup
A live source check found Universal credit at `ukpga/2012/5` section `1`. Use this as a locator, then check relevance and currency afresh. The check did not verify current in-force status. Do not substitute this provision for the legal question asked.

## Known conflicts and reform checks
Do not treat benefit reform announcements or review recommendations as enacted law. Northern Ireland and devolved Scottish benefits require separate checks.
