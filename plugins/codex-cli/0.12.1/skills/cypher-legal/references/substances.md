## When to use
Load for UK controlled-substance legal classification, possession/supply offences and statutory exemptions. Establish jurisdiction, exact substance/form, relevant date, conduct and any prescription or licence.

## Work the issue
Confirm the substance identity and legal instrument before classifying it. Distinguish possession, production, supply, import/export and regulatory licensing; verify schedules and amendments. A drug's health risk does not establish its statutory class, and medicinal availability does not create a general exemption.

## Starting lookup
